Both versions last 65 seconds and have no audio. The edited explainers contain an actual Duck.ai response from Claude Haiku 5.5, recorded on 9 November 2026. The example compares fitting a 3000 × 2000 pixel photo onto A4 landscape paper with filling the sheet. The large captions paraphrase the recorded answer.

The demo uses supplied research instructions within one browser chat. It does not show automatic skill installation or persistent memory. Those require a supported, connected agent environment. The browser record exposes a Japanese query and English/German sources; it does not expose every query the model claims to have run.

The videos are 1920 × 1080 at 25 fps, H.264/yuv420p. The last 15 seconds show the support card updated on 10 November 2026 with Swedbank, Stripe EUR/USD, PayPal, USDT BSC/BEP-20, USDT Solana/SPL and the author's Facebook profile. All six QR values were decoded from the encoded video and matched against the author-supplied values. The first 50 seconds retain the exact decoded frames of the earlier video. Verification receipt.
