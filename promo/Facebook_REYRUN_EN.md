I’m sharing REYRUN Recursive Deep Search, a free, open-source research skill for AI agents.

Project, download and researched examples: https://github.com/runcisreinis-glitch/reyrun-recursive-deep-search

Your agent receives a practical workflow: search in English, German, Chinese and Japanese; check sources; look for unfinished projects and practitioners’ experience; compare available solutions with new combinations that have a defensible advantage.

In a connected agent environment, the skill stores short cards describing successful and unsuccessful methods. The next task receives that experience. Changes to the procedure are tested before adoption. This does not guarantee that every answer will improve.

The project includes 30 actual research examples: 10 everyday questions, 10 medium-complexity questions across different fields and 10 difficult tasks. Seven include mathematical proofs and executed calculation code. The software mechanism suite contains 82 passing tests. A separate two-task demonstration shows a real agent completing both tasks and loading the first task’s saved lesson into the second. The short video also shows a real example in a free Duck.ai chat using Claude Haiku 5.5.

To get started, give your AI the project link and this instruction:

“Read SKILL.md and its references. If your environment supports skills, add this skill and verify that it works. Use it for my research task. State what you actually activated, and check whether the next task loads the saved experience.”

Persistent learning needs an agent environment that can retain files and run the included code. An ordinary chat can follow the research instructions its tools support.
