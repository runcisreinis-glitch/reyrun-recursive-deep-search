## Excluded material

The following categories are physically excluded from this edition:

- Private benchmark fixtures, baseline and instruction snapshots, and saved comparison-policy artifacts.
- Raw evaluator outputs, individual audit artifacts and internal review records.
- Begin, finish, handoff and follow-up execution records, agent or model telemetry, and execution metadata.
- Before/after memory snapshots, raw mechanism logs and internal verification or translation receipts.
- Tool-query dumps, query-batch captures, raw source/claim ledgers and duplicate intermediate exports.

These records have not been renamed or moved into another public file. The combined experiment report also omits the complete internal evaluator audits, historical and six-case audit artifacts, and the strict-host execution demonstration. The [published findings](PUBLISHED_FINDINGS.md) state their relevant reported outcomes and limitations in an authored account. Original private records remain separate from this public edition.

The three comparison tasks were designed for this project; they are not an external hidden benchmark. Their six research answers are published. The separate test-fixture, evaluation and execution records are excluded. Both methods met the stated answer checks, the candidate used 43 web actions against 47, the measured accuracy gain was zero, and the existing method was retained.

## Evidence limits

Readers can inspect all published answers, source links, mathematical reasoning, code and calculation output. This edition does not contain the raw records needed to replay the original private orchestration or independently inspect every intermediate evaluator action. The authored findings report the limited three-task comparison and the existing 82-test software result; they do not turn those checks into general search superiority, antivirus certification or a guarantee that each future answer improves.

The original query dumps, including records that once contained temporary signed download addresses or nonce/IP query parameters, are excluded entirely. No access token, signed-download credential, private machine path or private execution identifier is intentionally published. Ordinary research source URLs remain in the complete answers and public reference documents.

The approved author-support story and all payment details are preserved. The Latvian historical report and its combined copy now use the already approved Latvian story instead of the English version; no biography facts, amounts, addresses or support links were changed. This edition adds no new research trial and does not change the recorded `no_gain` result. A permanent learning cycle still requires a supported execution environment, persistent state and a configured worker; downloading the package alone does not create that service.
