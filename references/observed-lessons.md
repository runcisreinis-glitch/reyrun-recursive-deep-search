# Observed lessons from the release research

These are compact, source-linked observations from actual tasks, not promoted
claims of general quality improvement. Use a lesson only when its trigger fits
the current task, and recheck changing facts. The connected runtime remains the
authoritative record of a particular installation's learning and policy history.

The [selected 30-case report](../validation/2026-11-09-beta3/30-examples.lv.md)
and evidence index retain the
answers, source links, queries, calculation artifacts and original answer hashes.

The internal records referenced here are omitted from this edition; see [publication scope](../PUBLICATION_SCOPE.md).

| Trigger | Observed useful step | Evidence and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The same number appears beside different units or material states. | Name the quantity and its basis before arithmetic: volume versus mass, one coat versus two coats, dry versus cooked mass. | NE01–NE03. The later tasks loaded prior observations. This demonstrates transfer and arithmetic correctness, not a randomized estimate of the lesson's benefit. |
| A primary page opens but the relevant passage is absent. | Locate the actual answer, function, table note or model-specific clause; leave navigation-only content unread for the claim. | NP01–NP04. Some returned pages were incomplete or inaccessible and were excluded from support. Opening a page is not the same as reading its decisive content. |
| A certificate number seems to establish product performance. | Check the exact object, claimed reduction, conditions and the record's internal date. | NP04. A material-only component entry did not establish a finished filter's contaminant-removal performance. No physical filter was tested. |
| A percentage modifies an original dimension or a measured concentration. | Preserve the denominator and reverse-check the operation against the given values. | NP02–NP04. Fabric shrinkage uses division by the retained fraction; a reduction uses the pre-treatment value. Manufacturer examples do not override the task's inputs. |
| A cleaning result is being treated as a diagnosis. | Distinguish removable material from damage, state compatibility conditions, and keep a negative local check inconclusive. | NE04. The proposed glass check was researched, not physically performed. Historic or decorated glass had different treatment boundaries. |
| A discounted order is being compared only with allocated average cost. | Check incremental contribution, available capacity and displaced higher-margin work separately. | NP05. The stated spare-capacity case adds EUR90; the result changes if incremental overhead exceeds EUR90 or more than ten normal-price orders are displaced. This is a calculation under supplied inputs, not an audited business forecast. |
| An old and a current address share a street name or house number. | Bind the name to its period and street segment; establish building identity separately. | NP06. The 2024 Riga decision gives two successor segments for the old Maskavas street name. It does not prove an individual building's continuity or unchanged number. |
| No event was observed after an intervention. | Preserve observation coverage and exposure; distinguish zero observed events from zero underlying risk. | NP07. The zero-event calculation is explicitly hypothetical and does not estimate a particular window's collision risk. |
| A signal threshold is being treated as a full inverse-problem guarantee. | Separate amplified measurement error from the discarded or unobserved component. | NF01. A mode threshold and a retained-spectrum error bound do not bound the unknown high-frequency tail. |
| A numerical scheme matches one long-run statistic. | Check its other required dynamics and limiting cases separately. | NF02/NF03/NF06. A correct invariant variance can coexist with wrong autocorrelation; stable eigenvalues can coexist with transient growth or a defective boundary matrix. |
| A limiting argument discards mass on a shrinking set. | Prove the mode of convergence and inspect concentration at the boundary before exchanging limits and integrals. | NF05. The complete proof and the fixed-grid counterexample were executed; the result does not condemn every adaptive integrator. |
| A symbolic tool prints a group or theorem result. | Retain a finite arithmetic certificate and prove why its hypotheses imply the conclusion. | NF07. Factorizations, discriminant, prime conditions and the graph argument establish S5; the CAS group-name routine was not used as proof. |

## Retain, reject, discuss

Keep successful procedures with their scope. Keep a short record of failed
routes and the reason for failure, so they do not have to be rediscovered. An
unresolved idea remains a hypothesis with a specific next test. Compact memory
may remove acknowledged raw evidence only under the documented retention rules;
the release's retained evidence archive and public summaries serve different
purposes.

The separate prerequisite-ordering candidate has its own fixed comparison and
decision in the [release report](../validation/2026-11-09-beta3/release-report.lv.md).
It must not be treated as accepted merely because it sounds sensible or appears
in a candidate file. Read the actual comparison outcome before reusing it as a
default policy. The actual three-task pilot returned **no_gain**: both arms passed
all frozen answer checks, so the required precision gain was absent. The candidate
used 43 web actions versus 47, but its aligned elapsed time was higher. Neither
observation changes the preregistered acceptance objective. The baseline remained
active, and a subsequent ordinary run actually loaded the retained no-gain decision.
